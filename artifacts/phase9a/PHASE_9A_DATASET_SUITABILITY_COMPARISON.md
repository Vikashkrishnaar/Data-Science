# Phase 9A Dataset Suitability Comparison

## Decision lens

This comparison evaluates suitability for a **transformer-health / future-fault or failure-oriented use case**, while distinguishing targets that are actually supported by each release. “Future/lead-time target feasible” means that the documented release contains enough temporal, asset, event, and outcome structure to construct and evaluate that target without inventing joins or labels. Static diagnostic, event-classification, stability, and explicitly defined proxy tasks are reported separately and must not be presented as future-failure prediction.

## Ranked recommendation

1. **Retain the UK Power Station Transformer DGA dataset as the primary transformer-relevant candidate** for exploratory longitudinal analysis, next-observation DGA forecasting, regime-change/missingness handling, and explicitly labelled geomagnetic-storm response. It has the clearest combination of transformer identity, UTC timestamps, multi-year coverage, and repeated observations. It is **not** sufficient for supervised fault/failure forecasting because verified outcomes are absent.
2. **Retain the Wind Turbine SCADA dataset as a methodological early-warning reference**, not as a transformer dataset. It has the strongest documented event-label, asset-aware, and train/prediction structure, but its assets and domain are wind turbines and event starts are estimated.
3. **Use the Mendeley transformer DGA and IEEE DataPort membership-degree DGA datasets only for static diagnostic benchmarking or rule-reproduction audits.** Their transformer relevance is useful, but missing temporal/asset structure and method-derived labels prevent future-target claims.
4. **Use the transformer/ISPAR transients dataset only for simulated same-window event classification**, after archive-level verification of label mapping, schema, and licence. Do not infer field or future-failure performance.
5. **Use the 39-bus, Electrical Grid Stability, and related simulated stability datasets only for static or event-relative power-system stability benchmarks.** They do not provide transformer-health, asset-longitudinal, or future-failure evidence.

## Explicit target-feasibility decisions

- **Verified future transformer fault/failure target:** **No** for all eight candidates from the documented releases alone.
- **Transformer-relevant longitudinal/proxy target:** **Yes, conditionally, for the UK Power Station Transformer DGA dataset**; proxy targets must be named as proxies, not failures.
- **Retrospective event/anomaly early-warning target:** **Yes, conditionally, for the Wind Turbine SCADA dataset**, with domain and event-onset caveats.
- **Static diagnostic/event/stability targets:** **Yes** for the applicable candidates as identified in the table; these are different tasks from future failure forecasting.
- **Unknown fields:** Where the records state unknown row counts, raw schema, missingness, label mappings, or asset keys, those remain **unknown** and are not treated as affirmative evidence.

## Approval gate

**Gate status: Do not approve download, preprocessing, or modelling yet.** This Phase 9A synthesis used only the supplied verified candidate JSON. **No dataset download has occurred and no modelling, training, or performance evaluation has occurred.** Any next phase requires explicit approval plus candidate-specific verification, including archive/readme inspection, schema and row-count audit, timestamp and key validation, missingness/duplicate checks, leakage-safe split design, and confirmation of licences and label provenance. The UK DGA candidate should not be promoted to a supervised failure-forecasting benchmark without independent fault, maintenance, or outcome data.
